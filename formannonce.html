Form'Annonce

Overview

Form'Annonce is a B2B SaaS marketplace connecting French training organisations (Organismes de Formation) with independent trainers. It replaces the fragmented, Facebook-group-based recruitment process that dominates the sector with a structured, centralised platform.

Launched in March 2026, the platform reached 68+ users organically in under a month — no paid advertising, purely through LinkedIn outreach and Facebook prospecting. It operates as a PWA (installable on mobile and desktop) and is currently 100% free during its growth phase.

The Problem

France has approximately 144,000 registered training organisations and 90,000 independent trainers, 65% of whom work as subcontractors for larger organisations. Recruitment was almost entirely done through Facebook groups, direct messages, and word of mouth — disorganised, slow, and with no standardised process.

Form'Annonce centralises mission postings, candidate applications, document management, and communication notifications into a single product built specifically for this sector.

Technical Architecture

The stack was chosen for speed of iteration and zero infrastructure overhead:

  • Frontend: HTML, CSS, JS Vanilla — single-page application (SPA) with no framework, ES Modules via CDN. PWA with a Network First service worker.
  • Backend: Firebase — Hosting, Authentication (Google OAuth + Email), Firestore, Storage, Cloud Functions (Node.js v1).
  • Emails: Firebase Extension "Trigger Email" — all automated notifications (new application, accepted/refused status, weekly digest, mission closing) write to a mail collection that the extension processes.
  • Security: Strict Firestore rules (emails never exposed client-side), HTML sanitisation on all inputs, CSP headers, HMAC-signed unsubscribe links.

Key Features

  • Mission posting system for training organisations with full filtering (domain, department, modality, dates).
  • Application flow for independent trainers — CV upload, motivation, LinkedIn, portfolio documents.
  • Automated email notifications at every step of the hiring pipeline.
  • Weekly digest emails to trainers (filtered by their domains of expertise) and to organisations (new active trainers count).
  • "Organisme Vérifié" badge — manually granted after SIRET/NDA verification, stamped on all published missions.
  • "Pionnier" badge — automatically awarded to the first 200 users as a founding member reward, visible on profiles and applications.
  • Admin dashboard with user management, analytics, and moderation tools.
  • Open Graph dynamic previews for mission sharing on LinkedIn and WhatsApp via a dedicated Cloud Function.
  • GDPR-compliant account deletion (cascading cleanup of Firestore documents and Storage files).

Traction & Business

Form'Annonce is in active growth phase. The platform has an active partnership with FormDev (900+ training organisation clients, 3,000 daily users), enabling direct integration and distribution. The acquisition strategy is currently 100% organic — LinkedIn posts, Facebook group prospecting, and word of mouth.

The planned monetisation model introduces a Formateur Pro subscription (priority listing, instant alerts, candidature analytics) and an OF Pro plan (unlimited missions, trainer database access, Qualiopi-ready PDF exports) once sufficient traction is established.

Form'Annonce

Overview

Form'Annonce is a B2B SaaS marketplace connecting French training organisations (Organismes de Formation) with independent trainers. Most of the sector still recruits through Facebook groups; Form'Annonce replaces that with a structured, centralised platform.

Launched in March 2026, the platform reached 130+ users organically in under a month, with no paid advertising, just LinkedIn outreach and Facebook prospecting. It runs as a PWA (installable on mobile and desktop) and is currently free while it's still in its growth phase.

The Problem

France has approximately 144,000 registered training organisations and 90,000 independent trainers, 65% of whom work as subcontractors for larger organisations. Recruitment was almost entirely done through Facebook groups, direct messages, and word of mouth: disorganised, slow, and without any standardised process.

Form'Annonce centralises mission postings, candidate applications, document management, and communication notifications into a single product built specifically for this sector.

Technical Architecture

The stack was chosen for speed of iteration and zero infrastructure overhead:

  • Frontend: plain HTML, CSS and vanilla JS, no framework. Built as a single-page app with ES Modules loaded via CDN, and packaged as a PWA with a Network First service worker.
  • Backend: Firebase, covering Hosting, Authentication (Google OAuth + Email), Firestore, Storage and Cloud Functions (Node.js v1).
  • Emails: handled by the Firebase Extension "Trigger Email." All automated notifications (new application, accepted/refused status, weekly digest, mission closing) write to a mail collection that the extension picks up and sends.
  • Security: Strict Firestore rules (emails never exposed client-side), HTML sanitisation on all inputs, CSP headers, HMAC-signed unsubscribe links.

Key Features

  • Mission posting system for training organisations with full filtering (domain, department, modality, dates).
  • Application flow for independent trainers, including CV upload, motivation letter, LinkedIn profile and portfolio documents.
  • Automated email notifications at every step of the hiring pipeline.
  • Weekly digest emails to trainers (filtered by their domains of expertise) and to organisations (new active trainers count).
  • "Organisme Vérifié" badge, granted manually after SIRET/NDA verification and stamped on all published missions.
  • "Pionnier" badge, awarded automatically to the first 200 users as a founding member reward and shown on their profiles and applications.
  • Admin dashboard with user management, analytics, and moderation tools.
  • Open Graph dynamic previews for mission sharing on LinkedIn and WhatsApp via a dedicated Cloud Function.
  • GDPR-compliant account deletion (cascading cleanup of Firestore documents and Storage files).

Traction & Business

Form'Annonce is in an active growth phase. The platform has a partnership with FormDev (900+ training organisation clients, 3,000 daily users), which gives it direct integration and distribution. For now, growth stays entirely organic: LinkedIn posts, Facebook group prospecting and word of mouth.

The planned monetisation model introduces a Formateur Pro subscription (priority listing, instant alerts, candidature analytics) and an OF Pro plan (unlimited missions, trainer database access, Qualiopi-ready PDF exports) once sufficient traction is established.
